# A Brief History of Dragons

Dragons have appeared in human mythology for thousands of years. From the
Mesopotamian Mušḫuššu to the Chinese Lóng, these creatures symbolize power,
wisdom, and chaos.

## Western Dragons

In European folklore, dragons are typically depicted as fire-breathing
reptiles with wings and scales, often guarding treasure.

## Eastern Dragons

In East Asian traditions, dragons are benevolent, serpentine creatures
associated with water, weather, and imperial authority.

## Modern Depictions

Today, dragons remain popular in fantasy literature, games, and film,
continuing a tradition that spans millennia.
